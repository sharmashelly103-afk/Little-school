Baby Sparkle Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

In short: Baby Sparkle does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. It has no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no tracking.

What the app stores

Settings you choose (language, theme, font, music, game locks, screen-time limit) and the child profile you type in (a first name, a picture and an age) are saved only on your own device, in the app's local storage. They never leave the device. Removing the app deletes them.

Internet use

The app downloads its own pages, pictures and sound files from its website the first time it opens, so it can work offline afterwards. It does not send information about you or your child anywhere.

Children

Baby Sparkle is made for children aged 1 to 3, used together with a parent. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. A parent question appears when the app opens, and parents can lock games and set a screen-time limit in Settings.

Third parties

There are no third-party advertising, analytics or social-media services in the app.
